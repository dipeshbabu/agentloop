# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want AgentLoop to be a welcoming, safe, and constructive community. Everyone
who participates in good faith receives the same opportunity to take part,
regardless of background, identity, experience, education, ability, appearance,
nationality, economic position, philosophy, or religion.

This commitment applies to maintainers, contributors, users, and anyone
representing the project.

## Expected behavior

Community members are expected to:

- communicate with kindness, patience, and honesty;
- respect different viewpoints and experiences;
- give and receive constructive technical feedback;
- take responsibility for mistakes and work to repair harm;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, and clearly stated boundaries; and
- keep discussions focused on the project and the well-being of its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, stalking, or unwanted personal attention;
- insulting, demeaning, discriminatory, or sexualized comments or conduct;
- sharing another person's private information without permission;
- deliberate intimidation, disruption, impersonation, or evasion of moderation;
- presenting someone else's work without appropriate credit; or
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern or assists an investigation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repositories, issue trackers, reviews,
discussions, events, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone is
officially representing AgentLoop in public.

## Reporting a concern

Report a possible violation privately to the project lead using the contact
information on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/dipeshbabu).
Do not put sensitive details in a public issue. If no private contact method is
available, open a minimal issue asking for a private reporting channel without
naming people or describing the incident.

Reports should include, when possible, what happened, where it happened, relevant
context or records, and any immediate safety concern. The maintainers will limit
information to people who need it to assess and resolve the report. A maintainer
who is named in a report will not take part in deciding its outcome. If a report
concerns the only maintainer, reporters may also use the host platform's abuse
reporting process.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may use any of these responses, depending on the impact and pattern
of the behavior:

1. A private clarification or warning, with an opportunity to correct the harm.
2. A temporary limit on participation or contact.
3. A temporary suspension from project spaces.
4. A permanent ban for severe behavior or a pattern that other steps did not
   resolve.

Enforcement decisions will be made privately, documented, and applied as
consistently as possible. The safety and privacy of affected people take
priority. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that
do not follow this Code of Conduct.
